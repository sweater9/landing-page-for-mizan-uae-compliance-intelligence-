Before you begin

A few useful answers.

What is Mizan designed for?

Mizan brings together UAE regulatory research and profile-driven compliance planning. It is designed to help organize research, inspect sources and identify follow-up questions.

Is this a production compliance service?

The repository describes Mizan as a full-stack demonstration. Some data, dates, profiles and planning labels may be illustrative. Check each record’s evidence and review status; this page does not certify the application’s data or availability.

Does Mizan replace legal or compliance advice?

No. Confirm applicable requirements and current consolidated texts with official authorities, and seek qualified advice where needed. Mizan supports research and planning.

Where can I explore the application and source code?

Open the Mizan application or review the application repository on GitHub. The hosted service may take a moment to become available.
